Institutional review required. These terms describe current product behaviour. A college or public authority must adapt the responsible organisation, complaints and statutory wording before launch.

Using the service

You may browse public reports without an account. A verified email account is needed to publish, save, claim, message or use account features. Keep your email account secure and sign out of shared devices.

Accurate, respectful reports

  • Publish only items you lost, found or are authorised to report.
  • Use an approximate public place, never a home address or precise private address.
  • Keep ownership proof, document numbers, payment information and sensitive details private.
  • For shopping-app reference screenshots, upload only material you may share and label it as a reference—not proof of ownership.
  • Do not impersonate, harass, mislead, advertise, defraud or use the service for illegal activity.

Claims and returns

A match suggestion, similarity label or location proximity is not proof of ownership. Use private verification questions and meet in a busy public place. Do not send payments, passwords, OTPs, full identity numbers or banking details through the app. The service does not process payments, act as escrow or guarantee a handover.

Both participants confirm a completed return before a report is marked returned. Items in college custody follow the college collection procedure.

Moderation and safety

Users can report a listing and block a participant. Authorised operators may hide or restore content or restrict access for safety, misuse prevention, legal compliance or investigation. Operators must not inspect unrelated private information.

Availability and changes

The service depends on hosting, email, storage and network providers. Free hosting may wake after inactivity, so the first request can be delayed. We do not promise uninterrupted availability, successful matches, inbox delivery or recovery of every item.

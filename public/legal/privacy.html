Privacy notice

Your details should help a return, not become the story.

Effective 17 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Institutional review required. This operational notice describes the current application. A college or public authority must have its legal/privacy officer review it, name the responsible organisation and publish local contact and grievance details before launch.

What this covers

Lost & Found helps people publish lost or found-item reports, receive possible matches, exchange private claim messages and record returns. It applies to the website, installed web app and direct Android launcher that open the same service.

Public and private information

Open reports can show item details, report date, category, approximate location and chosen images. Never put phone numbers, email addresses, document numbers, home addresses, order numbers, payment details or identity documents in public fields or reference screenshots.

Use current location stores a rounded approximate area and discards the precise device fix. Coordinate matching is limited to 0–200 metres; the displayed approximate area remains public.

Private notes, claim evidence and messages are not shown in public listings or used as public matching text. They are available only to relevant participants and authorised college staff handling custody, verification, safety or moderation.

Use, sharing and security

Information is used to run the service, protect it from misuse, suggest matches, administer returns and meet applicable legal or safety obligations. We do not sell personal data or use report content for behavioural advertising.

Hosted database, private photo-storage, email, backup and source-control providers process data only as needed to operate the service. Access is limited to configured service accounts and authorised operators. A college deployment must document its providers and any applicable transfer assessment.

Sign-in uses a time-limited email code; the app does not ask users to create an application password. Sessions use secure cookies on HTTPS. Images are checked and location metadata is removed. Backups are encrypted and restore-checked. No service can promise absolute security.

Your requests

You can edit, close or delete eligible reports. Deleting a report removes it and linked claims/messages from the active service where return/custody safeguards allow it. It cannot recall copies downloaded or screenshotted by others. Encrypted backups can retain deleted information until their retention window ends; see Data retention.

Data retention

Keep what helps a return. Remove what no longer does.

Effective 17 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Institutional review required. This policy states current technical defaults. A deploying institution must approve its legal holds, records schedule, complaint handling and retention requirements.

Deletion and institutional holds

Deleting a report removes it from the active application where return/custody safeguards allow it. A deletion cannot remove copies already downloaded or captured by another person, and encrypted backups may retain information until their retention window ends.

Items in college custody may require separate records under the institution’s approved schedule. Staff must not bypass a legal hold, investigation, statutory records duty or emergency direction.

# College staff operations guide

Effective 17 November 2026. This guide is for authorised Lost & Found staff. It describes current product controls; it is not a substitute for institutional privacy, safety, accessibility, records or legal policy.

## Before access

1. Designate a service owner, privacy/grievance contact, moderation lead and incident contact.
2. Grant access only after training and remove it when a role changes.
3. Approve the [Privacy notice](../public/legal/privacy.html), [Terms](../public/legal/terms.html) and [Data retention policy](../public/legal/retention.html) with the institution’s contacts and records rules.
4. Confirm the latest encrypted backup/restore check and email service health.

## Daily checklist

- Review new flags, unsafe listings and pending staff-reviewed claims.
- Check custody items, collection-code activity and unresolved return issues.
- Check the operations panel for repeated failures, backup status and worker alerts.
- Never put OTPs, passwords, private evidence or photo links in tickets, chat channels or public GitHub issues.

## Item in college custody

1. Record only the item and safe storage reference. Never publish serial numbers, document numbers, contents or precise storage locations.
2. Ask the claimant a private question based on information absent from the public report.
3. Check evidence and claim context. Never approve your own claim or rely on a match score alone.
4. Arrange collection at a staffed public desk and use the collection-code workflow where available.
5. Record the handover through the app. Do not mark returned before required confirmation.

## Privacy and safety

- Access private evidence, messages and custody details only for an assigned return, safety, moderation or legal task.
- Do not export, print or share private details unless the approved procedure requires it.
- Use approximate public locations; never request home addresses or payment details.
- Do not mediate payments or direct people to isolated meetings.
- Escalate impersonation, account compromise, data exposure, threats or harassment immediately.

## Moderation, deletion and incidents

Owners can delete eligible reports. Staff should hide unsafe, unlawful, misleading or reviewed listings; do not delete user content merely to resolve a dispute. Custody/return safeguards require the approved staff process before removal.

For an incident, preserve only the minimum necessary information, restrict access if needed, record time/operator/report IDs in the approved incident record, notify the service owner and privacy contact, and escalate immediate danger to campus security or emergency services.

## Training sign-off

Before access, each staff member should demonstrate public/private data boundaries, private claim verification, custody controls, hide/restore, phishing/OTP awareness and safe incident escalation.
